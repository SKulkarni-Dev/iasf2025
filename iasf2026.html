About IASF 2026

EduAIthon 2026 was one of the flagship innovation events conducted alongside the IUCEE Annual Student Forum (IASF 2026) and ICTIEE 2026 at GITAM University, Bengaluru. It was designed as a national hackathon where students and faculty collaborated to create AI-powered educational solutions. Theme: "Empowering Self-Learning through AI" The challenge focused on developing personalized AI learning tools that help students learn engineering course topics independently using Generative AI and modern educational technologies.
