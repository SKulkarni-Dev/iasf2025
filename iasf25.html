About IASF 2025

The IUCEE Annual Student Forum (IASF) 2025 was a remarkable gathering of aspiring engineers, innovators, educators, and industry professionals from across the country. The forum served as a platform for students to exchange ideas, develop leadership qualities, and explore innovative solutions to real-world challenges through collaboration and interdisciplinary learning.

Hosted at VNR Vignana Jyothi Institute of Engineering and Technology, Hyderabad, the event featured inspiring keynote sessions, technical workshops, panel discussions, project showcases, and networking opportunities with renowned academicians and industry experts. Participants gained valuable insights into emerging technologies, sustainable engineering practices, entrepreneurship, and the evolving role of engineers in shaping society.

Throughout the forum, students collaborated with peers from diverse institutions, strengthened their problem-solving abilities, and embraced the values of innovation, teamwork, and social responsibility. The experience encouraged participants to think beyond classrooms, transforming ideas into impactful solutions that contribute to a sustainable and technology-driven future.

For the IUCEE Student Chapter at KLE Technological University, IASF 2025 was more than just an event—it was an unforgettable journey of learning, inspiration, and meaningful connections. Every interaction, workshop, and shared experience strengthened our commitment to fostering innovation, leadership, and collaborative growth within our student community.
